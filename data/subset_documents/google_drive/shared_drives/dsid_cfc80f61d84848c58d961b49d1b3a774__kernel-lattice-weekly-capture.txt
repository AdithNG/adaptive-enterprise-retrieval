Experiment notes
- Kernel-lattice tuning knobs tried this week:
  - affinity-window=25ms (reduced preemption) -> smoother tail but small p50 increase
  - tilt-factor=0.8 (prioritize older requests) -> reduced starvation in low-load tests
  - coalesce-threshold=6 -> reduced tiny-batch overhead
- KV-cache variants:
  - LRU-tenant-aware prototype: improved reserved-tenant hit rate to 0.42 (from 0.18) in local replay
  - Prefill-coalescer changes: prefill windows aligned at 100ms showed better aggregation but increased memory transient by ~6%
