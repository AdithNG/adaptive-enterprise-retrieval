Objective:
- Quick scout for Aureon Health (regional hospital chain + telehealth). See if we can get a 90-day VPC pilot for automated patient intake and discharge summary drafting (PHI = concern).

Snapshot:

- Size: ~18 hospitals, 2M annual visits (estimate from investor deck). Likely ARR potential: $150k-$400k/year depending on scope (pilot -> prod).

Key contacts (to find emails/LinkedIn):- Chief Digital Officer (CDO) — likely Sara Whitman (title variants in org chart) - CIO — Marco DeLuca - Director of Clinical Informatics — probable champion - Telehealth product manager (PM) — outreach to identify use cases

Hypotheses / Why Redwood fits:
- PHI -> need private deployment (VPC or on-prem). Redwood Private + KMS/HSM fits ask.
- Need low-latency for real-time intake -> Dedicated pools recommended.
- Value prop: reduce clinician touch time, consistent summary templates, cost predictability vs building in-house.

Pilot suggestion (90 days):
- Scope: intake transcription -> draft discharge summary (template-driven) for 3 clinics.
- Infra: Redwood Private VPC with dedicated capacity (1-2 gpus reserved), HSM for keys, 30-day logs retention (can increase).
- Success metrics: ~30% reduction in clinician edit time, >85% template-compliance, feedback loop for model tuning.
- Data flow: transcripts (de-identified copy) -> modeling in private network; no storage outside customer region.
- Minimal engineering lift: one integration webhook + small adapter service to Epic export.
