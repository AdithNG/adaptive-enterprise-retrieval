2) Cost predictability: show monthly cost modeling for token volumes + recommendations from Redwood Optimize (batching + caching) to reduce per-query cost vs their current per-ticket agent cost.
3) Quality + control: ability to pin model versions and roll out via canary; private deployment option for PII-sensitive flows (order numbers, addresses).
4) Quick pilot w/ measurable KPI: deflection %, avg handle time saved, # of escalations prevented.

Pilot hypothesis and scope (minimum viable pilot):
- Scope: implement Redwood hosted or dedicated endpoint for 3 intents: order status, returns initiation, and shipping ETA. Integrate via Zendesk webhook or small middleware to intercept chat messages.
- Duration: 6 weeks (2 weeks integration + training, 4 weeks live A/B with 10% traffic).
- Success metrics: 20%+ deflection (self-serve resolution), 15% decrease in AHT for agent-handled tickets, 90%+ intent accuracy on curated prompt set.
- Rollout trigger: if deflection >= 20% and NPS impact neutral/positive, expand to 25% traffic in next phase.
