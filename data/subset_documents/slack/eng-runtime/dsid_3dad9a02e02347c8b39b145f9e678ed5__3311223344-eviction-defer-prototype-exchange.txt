ryan: pretty convincing. When selector chooses fused_attn_v2 it needs larger scratchpad windows. Our slab allocator always eagerly reclaims LRU which causes premature eviction of hot activations.
priya: If activations are reloaded from KV cache after eviction, that explains repeat work and perf regression.
anya: Proposal: add a short "eviction-defer" grace window when kernel change happens (say 200ms) and also pin KV-backed activations for that window. That prevents immediate LRU eviction on transient spikes.
zed: sounds plausible. Risks: increased peak memory if spike sustained -> real OOM.
ryan: We can implement it as adaptive: defer eviction only if peak > baseline by X% and predicted retention benefit > Y.
priya: also couple to batching heuristics: if batcher is about to split (B>threshold) then we may avoid deferring because workload is sustained. Otherwise defer for transient kernel-induced spikes.
