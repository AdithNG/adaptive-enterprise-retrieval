Dan S.: For ALB specifically we often see ALB idle timeout (default 60s) close connections if there's >60s between packets. Two options: increase idle timeout to > server heartbeat interval OR push keepalive heartbeats at shorter interval. Also make sure the origin sends some bytes (e.g., ":\n\n" SSE comment heartbeat) before the ALB idle.

Priya S.: Customer says intermittent partial output earlier than 60s though — sometimes after 15-25s. Could be Cloudflare layer. Cloudflare can buffer responses for certain routes, and their edge may close if it thinks connection idle.

Setting proper headers helps: ensure Transfer-Encoding: chunked and disable Content-Length, and add Cache-Control: no-transform. Also try toggling "Connection: keep-alive" behavior.
