Alex / Soojin — quick update from infra and runtime after triage: we've identified a traffic shift on the us-east realtime cluster that increased inter-request batching latency for streaming clients pinned to a small set of edge nodes. Root cause appears to be a combination of a recent experiment that broadened batch windows for efficiency plus an edge routing policy that concentrated ApexSoft sessions to a hot subset of nodes.

Actions we performed (platform-side):
- Routing tweak: rebalanced ApexSoft sessions away from the hot subset to additional edge pools (small, safe change) — immediate improvement observed.
- Reverted the batch-window experiment for the affected pool (reduced batch window from 40ms back to 10ms for streaming paths).
- Warmed KV caches for the affected shards and cleared a stale kernel selection flag that was causing unnecessary path selection overhead.

Customer guidance (client-side) we still recommend:
1) Apply streaming.chunk_ms=1500 temporarily to smooth UI rendering during residual variance.
2) For critical flows, use the non-streaming finite-response call until you can validate stability in the next 24 hours.
3) If you can, route a small percentage of traffic to region=us-west-1 as a quick A/B.
