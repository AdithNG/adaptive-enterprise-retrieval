Hi Elaine / Karthik — copied in from CS. A couple of operational points that matter to pricing and how we structure concessions: 

- Usage variance: for customers with seasonal spikes we recommend a staged ramp with a quarterly true-up rather than a strict fixed-annual allotment — it reduces over/under-credit risk.
- Onboarding credits: we typically include a one-time onboarding credit (applies to consumption in months 1–3) to offset early setup costs.
- Expansion credits: we can offer a % of net-new consumption in year 2 that converts to credits against future invoices (usually 3–10% depending on term).

I'll attach our onboarding ramp template so you can see how the step-ups look across quarters. Happy to run the sample numbers once Karthik shares the committed quantity.
