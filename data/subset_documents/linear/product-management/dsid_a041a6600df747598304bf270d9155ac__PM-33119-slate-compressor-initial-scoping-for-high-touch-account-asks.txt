Slate Compressor: initial scoping and prioritization for high-touch account asks

Problem: Sales and CS funnel an asynchronous flood of feature asks from high-touch enterprise prospects. Current first-pass triage is slow, inconsistent, and produces large, disparate tickets that burden PM and Eng review cycles. Goal: Create a lightweight, repeatable 'Slate Compressor' intake flow that aggregates related asks into prioritized "slates" for rapid economic sizing and decisioning. Outcomes: (1) ~40% reduction in initial review volume for PMs, (2) consistent trade-off frames (cost/latency/risk/regulatory), (3) fast handoff docs for Solutions/Eng to scope and estimate.
Incoming enterprise asks are bucketed into a Slate within 48 hours of CS submission
Each Slate has a canonical one-paragraph problem statement, 3 prioritized solution options, and a first-pass econ delta (low/med/high)
PM lead can decide Go/No-Go for implementation vs pilot vs nurture within one cycle
Templates and playbook are documented and used by at least two CS teams as measured by intake form adoption
Draft Slate Compressor intake form (fields: account, ask cluster tags, business outcome, rough econ, compliance constraints, urgency)
