Centralize enterprise identity & admin request intake and prioritization rubric

Goal: Build a single, repeatable intake and prioritization process for enterprise admin & identity requests (RBAC, SSO/SAML, SCIM provisioning, org policies) so we can consistently route work to self-serve, docs, engineering, or enterprise track and accelerate time-to-resolution for high-risk customers.

Scope: non-bug feature/ops requests from customers or sales/CS that relate to access, identity, org-level policies, or admin tooling. This ticket covers: intake form fields, a scoring-based prioritization rubric, routing rules for next-step handling, and a lightweight staff playbook for evaluation and decision-making. Does NOT include implementation tasks (those will be child tickets).

Why now: We have increasing enterprise demand for identity integrations and strict access controls. Current handling is ad hoc across CS, SRE, and engineering which causes inconsistent SLAs and missed security dependencies. A centralized triage + rubric will reduce escalations, speed approvals for high-value accounts, and clarify what is building vs. doc/self-serve.

Success metrics: 1) Reduce time from intake submission to assigned owner to <72 hours for high-severity enterprise requests; 2) 60% of incoming requests routed to self-serve/doc guidance or product backlog without ad-hoc engineering rework; 3) Clear decision logging for 100% of enterprise requests (owner + rationale).

Expected deliverables: intake form template (Zendesk/HubSpot/Confluence + API mapping), triage rubric with scoring thresholds, routing matrix, staff playbook (triage steps + security signoff), and a small pilot with CS and two enterprise accounts.
A standardized intake form saved to Confluence and integrated with our support pipeline (example mapping to Zendesk fields)
A documented scoring rubric with thresholds for auto-route to self-serve, product backlog, enterprise-track engineering, or security review
Routing matrix that specifies owner (CS, PM, Engineering, Security), expected SLAs, and follow-up channels
Pilot run with two enterprise customers and data captured on time-to-assignment and routing accuracy
Playbook exists and is shared with CS, Solutions, and Support triage teams with at least one training session completed
Scoring dimensions (0-5 each): Customer size & ARR impact, Regulatory/compliance requirement, Security exposure/risk, Service continuity impact (SLOs), Implementation complexity (engineering effort)
Priority score = weighted sum: ARR*0.30 + Compliance*0.25 + Security*0.20 + SLOs*0.15 + Complexity*0.10
Routing thresholds: 0-6 -> Self-serve / Docs; 7-11 -> Product backlog (standard sprint queue); 12-16 -> Enterprise track (reserved capacity / customer-specific work); 17-25 -> Urgent + Security review & exec escalation
Auto-escalation triggers: explicit regulatory requirement (GDPR/Data residency/PCI/CCPA), manifest SSO failure affecting >50 users, or SCIM provisioning causing account lockouts
Required reviewers: For scores >=12 require PM + Engineering IC + Security rep sign-off (email + Confluence decision log)
