Private session-stitching race causes SAML assertion replay and RBAC binder mismatch, audit gaps during KMS rotation

Multiple on-prem customers reported elevated and inconsistent privilege elevation following a scheduled KMS envelope rotation. Symptoms: short-lived sessions where users from Tenant A temporarily surfaced as Tenant B, RBAC binder applied incorrect tenant mappings for ~5-15 minute windows, and audit ingestion dropped ~12 minutes of events in the control plane. This ticket captures investigation, provisional mitigations, and the urgent patch and rollout plan.

A race between the private SSO proxy's assertion cache (session-stitching path) and the KMS envelope pointer swap led to stale assertion lookup keys being returned for new sessions. The cache key used a truncated NameID and session timestamp which collided under rotation timing, enabling replayed assertions to be accepted and processed by the RBAC binder. Separately, a non-atomic KMS envelope pointer update caused the audit ingestion layer to filter out events whose envelope IDs did not match expected headers, producing the audit gap.
