Cerulean Support Networks

Summary: Cerulean is a B2B contact center operator (outsourced support across retail + fintech customers). They need a Dedicated deployment to guarantee 24/7 capacity for agent-assist and automatic ticket summarization. Primary driver is reliability during spikes (seasonal retail peaks + fintech month-ends) and strict data residency for EU/US customers.

Key requirements / quotes: 
- "We cannot have agent context timeouts when volume jumps — must have reserved throughput and predictable fallbacks." — Head of Support Ops (R. Delgado)
- 2000 concurrent active agents at peak, target p95 latency <120-200ms for streaming suggestions, sustained throughput budgeted ~40k-70k inference requests/minute (mix of short prompts + long wrap-up generations).
- Need isolated capacity (VPC) with explicit burst policy: baseline reserved QPS with ability to burst 2-3x for 30-60 min windows.
- Use cases: real-time agent suggestions (inline in Zendesk), end-of-interaction ticket summarization (concise, 3-line TL;DR + suggested tags), tool-calling to trigger ticket escalations / CRM field updates, automated quick-replies for chat channels.
- Integration: Zendesk + Salesforce connectors required; plan to swap to SFDC-case routing for enterprise customers.
- Metrics for acceptance: p95 latency under 200ms for agent-assist, fallback rate <1%, summarization F1 against human references >0.82, cost per 1k requests within agreed threshold.
- Security/compliance: SSO/SAML (Okta), SOC2 type II, audit logging with 12-month retention, KMS-managed keys, fine-grained retention controls per customer region. Legal insists on subprocessor list and right-to-audit clause.
- Observability needs: token-level cost breakdown, per-route latency dashboards, root-cause traces for spike incidents. Must integrate with their SRE PagerDuty rota.

POC notes and findings: 
- Pilot exposed two issues: 1) short KV cache TTL causing repeated context tokenization; recommended caching config reduced token cost by 22% during pilot. 2) morning shift spike (09:00–10:00 PST) produced throttling on shared pool — motivates Dedicated reserved capacity.
- SE recommendation: Dedicated pool with autoscale disabled for baseline + scheduled burst windows; pre-warm KV caches and enable prefix caching for common templates.

Commercial / timeline: 
- Procurement aiming to sign for Q2 2025 (forecast 2025-03).
- Estimated ARR ask for proposal: $250k–$750k depending on burst SLA and on-prem connector.
