Structured audit export, PII redaction evidence, and intake for privacy-scan SIEM ingest

Issue summary: Customer requests a one-time export of structured audit events (API calls, tokenized user identifiers, key management events) covering the past 12 months plus a rolling 90-day stream mapping for ingestion into their privacy scanning SIEM. They require evidence that PII fields have been redacted consistently and a manifest showing checksums and chain-of-custody for delivered artifacts.

Impact: This is an enterprise compliance milestone tied to a scheduled privacy audit with BrightHealth AI's legal team. Without the export and redaction evidence they cannot complete their external privacy attestation.

Requested artifacts and format:
- Monthly full bulk export (12 months) as NDJSON compressed tarball (gz) with index manifest (sha256 checksums).
- Rolling 90-day streaming export as NDJSON slices (1-day blobs) in S3 with lifecycle policy.
- Mapping file from Redwood audit fields to Elastic Common Schema (ECS) names.
- Redaction report describing which fields were redacted and the algorithm used (deterministic tokenization vs hashing), sample of 10 anonymized records showing before/after.
- Chain-of-custody manifest (timestamped, signed by Redwood service account) and delivery audit log showing who initiated and confirmed export.

Background/Constraints: BrightHealth AI handles PHI and is subject to additional privacy scrutiny; they cannot receive raw PII. They prefer not to have SSNs or email addresses present in any form. They are open to deterministic tokenization that preserves linkage (stable pseudonyms) but need documentation and an explanation of re-identification risk.

Preferred delivery: Pre-signed S3 URLs (expiring link) for each artifact plus a single zipped evidence bundle. Provide an estimated size and ETA for each deliverable.

Acceptance criteria:
- All artifacts delivered to pre-signed S3 with checksums and manifest.
- Redaction report approved by BrightHealth AI security contact.
- SIEM mapping document validated by customer (comments addressed).

Notes: Internal runbook for large exports suggests chunking by week to avoid timeouts; export job may need quota bump for S3 put traffic.
