Post-close rate correction removed hourly buffer credits causing provisional negative balance

Issue summary:
Customer BrightPages Inc. observed a negative provisional balance after a retroactive rate correction was applied post-invoice finalization for a 4-hour window on 2026-03-08 UTC. The rate correction removed previously-applied hourly buffer credits for the window 2026-03-08T02:00Z - 2026-03-08T06:00Z, causing a ~-$1,250 provisional swing on their account.

Impact:
- BrightPages reports a provisional negative balance that blocked autopay for their enterprise reserved contract.
- A small set (~350) of hourly postbacks were reclassified from "hourly-buffer" to "hosted-rate" during the retrospective rerate.
- Immediate business impact: invoice autopay held; customer unable to deploy a scheduled job that checks for invoice status due to webhook failure (dependent system).

Environment:
- prod-us-east (dedicated pool for BrightPages)
- Affected models: redwood-13b and redwood-7b-quant used by their batch embedding and chat endpoints.

Steps to reproduce / observed timeline:
1) 2026-03-08 07:30 UTC: accounting runs retroactive-rate-reconciler for last week (automated job).
2) 2026-03-08 07:35 UTC: reconciler applied a rate-correction patch (PR #4432) which modified rate tags for certain postback classes.
3) 2026-03-08 07:36 UTC: postbacks in the hourly buffer window were re-tagged and removed from the buffer credits bucket.
4) 2026-03-09 04:20 UTC: BrightPages' autopay attempt failed due to provisional negative balance alert.
