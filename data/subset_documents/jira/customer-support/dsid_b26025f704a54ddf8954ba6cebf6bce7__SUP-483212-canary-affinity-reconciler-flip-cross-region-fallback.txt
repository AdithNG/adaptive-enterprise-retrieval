Pinned-session reconciler flips TTL during canary weight increase causing cross-region fallback and degraded responses

Issue summary:
During a scheduled canary weight increase for redwood-open-13B-v2, a subset of pinned sessions belonging to FinSight Analytics were observed to lose their long-lived pin and instead receive a short-lived transient pin from the affinity reconciler. This triggered cross-region fallback routing to a lower-capacity quantized variant and caused degraded latency and truncated streaming responses for ~2% of requests from the customer for ~45 minutes.

Impact:
- Customer-facing inference errors: increased latency, partial responses (truncated stream) for a subset of pinned sessions.
- Estimated ~2% of FinSight production requests affected between 2026-02-25T18:12Z and 2026-02-25T18:57Z.
- No data exfiltration. No 5xx rate spike for the global tenant, but elevated 5xx/stream-drops per-session on affected route.

Environment:
- Prod cluster: us-east primary, eu-west failover.
- Canary rollout: weight shift from 5% -> 25% for redwood-open-13B-v2 at 2026-02-25T18:10Z.
- Traffic type: low-latency chat (streaming enabled).

Steps to reproduce (internal):
1) Create pinned session with pin TTL set by reconciler (long-lived) on route /v1/chat for customer FinSight.
2) Trigger canary weight increase from 5% -> 25% in us-east.
3) Observe affinity reconciler logs for token reconciliation and prefix-cache warming events.
4) Reproduce traffic pattern that hits prefix-cache warm path and streaming boundary conditions.

Observed behavior:
- Affinity reconciler emitted a 'pin-flip' event, updating token metadata TTL to a short transient value (~30s) for an active cohort.
- Router then evaluated the session as eligible for fallback routing and selected redwood-open-13B-v2-quant in eu-west for a small subset (sticky tokens were re-evaluated).
- Streaming responses were truncated due to mismatched max-seq and kernel config on the quantized variant.

Expected behavior:
- Pinned session TTL should be preserved during canary weight shifts; reconciler should not shorten pinned TTL for already-active sessions.
- Router should preserve session affinity to pinned variant unless explicit pin expiry occurs.

Logs/traces: (excerpts)
- routing-reconciler[18:12:34Z] INFO "reconcile: token=abc123 cohort=fin-fo-42 prior_ttl=3600 new_ttl=30 reason=canary-weight-sync"
- serving-runtime[18:12:36Z] WARN "fallback-selected model=redwood-open-13B-v2-quant region=eu-west session=abc123"
- stream-proxy[18:12:38Z] ERROR "stream_drop: reason=unexpected-eos kernel-mismatch"

Temporary workaround provided to customer:
- Support enabled an explicit session pin override for FinSight tenant to force long-lived TTL on /v1/chat routes while a fix was developed.

Next steps:
- Engineering to patch reconciler logic to prevent TTL compression during weight adjustments.
- Add unit/integration test for canary weight transitions with pinned sessions.
- Monitor affected metrics and customer reports for 72 hours post-deploy.

Affinity reconciler included a TTL-compression heuristic to reduce prefix-cache entries during canary syncs. The heuristic failed to check active-session flag and shortened TTL for some active pinned tokens during a weight increase, allowing router to treat them as expired and route to fallback variants.
